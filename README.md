# imprint

imprint helps you write to NFC (near field communication) cards and tags from your phone, on Android and iOS.

- **Write** a link, phone number or SMS to a card, so any phone can open it with a tap.
- **Read** what's on a card, and see how much space is used and free.
- **Password** to stop anyone else changing or erasing a card.
- **Instant Open** to open links straight in Chrome on Android.

Writing works on imprint cards. Other cards can still be read.
